Case study · Hospitality

A churrascaria's ritual, in six languages.

Fogo de Chão opened its second Santa Cruz restaurant in the new Mall Ventura Sur. I built a warm, editorial site that explains the gaúcho ritual, shows every cut, and turns visits into WhatsApp reservations, in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German and Chinese.

01 · The brief

A rodízio has rules that first-time guests don't know. The cuts keep coming to the table, the Market Table is self-service, and a two-sided card tells the gauchos when to bring more and when to pause.

The new restaurant also needed a home for its weekly specials and events, and an easy way to reserve a table for locals, business travelers and tourists alike.

02 · The approach

  1. Explain the ritualThe home page walks guests through the evening in order: the ritual in three steps, the rodízio cuts, the Market Table, the card, the bar, the week's specials and, finally, their table.
  2. Every cut has a pagePicanha, fraldinha, bife ancho, tomahawk, T-bone, costela and alcatra each get tasting notes, the recommended doneness, where the cut comes from and a wine pairing.
  3. Book where people already areThe reservation page asks for the date, time and number of guests, then opens WhatsApp with the request already written. FOGO, a chat host, handles bookings, group events, menu questions and everything else.

03 · What I built

  • Six languages, each with its own web address
  • Menu, cuts, weekly specials, events and location pages
  • A detail page for every cut, with tasting notes and wine pairings
  • Reservation form that sends the request to WhatsApp
  • FOGO chat host for bookings and questions
  • Semana Fogo: the week's offers, with today highlighted
  • A content manager so the team updates the menu, specials and photos
  • Directions to the mall, one tap from every page
