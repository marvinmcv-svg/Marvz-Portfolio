Case study · Equestrian

Three decades of horse breeding, told like a film.

Haras La Alhambra has bred Purebred Arabian and Pura Raza Española horses in Urubó, Santa Cruz, since 1993. I designed and built a cinematic, bilingual site that tells that story in eight chapters, brings people to the farm for a visit, and answers their questions at any hour with an AI assistant.

Visit the live site ↗

01 · The brief

La Alhambra is more than a stud farm. It breeds three bloodlines, runs a riding school for children from four years old and for adults, offers boarding and training, and hosts evening galas and the Santa Cruz dressage circuit in its own arena.

All of that had to feel as premium as the horses. And every kind of visitor, whether a buyer, a rider looking for a trainer or a parent looking for lessons, needed a clear path to their part of the farm and to a visit.

02 · The approach

  1. Story firstI organized the site as eight numbered chapters: Origin, Bloodlines, Philosophy, The Haras, Nights, Education, Services & rates, and Visit us. The home page plays like a trailer, and each chapter has its own page for people who want more.
  2. Art directionThe opening film plays through the cut-out letters of ALHAMBRA. A classic serif, a warm palette and the farm's own photography carry through every page, with scroll-driven reveals, a sideways bloodline gallery and “A day at La Alhambra” from 07:00 to 21:00.
  3. Answers without a phone callSpirit, an AI assistant trained on the farm's information, answers questions about the horses, lessons, rates, events and visits, with quick replies for the common ones. Rates are published openly, starting with a Bs 150 trial class.

03 · What I built

  • Eight-chapter site with a page for each chapter
  • Spanish and English throughout
  • Bloodline gallery for Arabian, PRE and Hispano-Arabian horses
  • Riding school with four levels, from Pony Club to classical dressage
  • Services and rates: boarding, training, arenas and paddocks
  • Events page with the 2025 dressage circuit
  • Spirit, the AI assistant, on every page
  • Visit section with Instagram contact and directions
