Case study · Restaurants · SaaS

A restaurant system built for how Bolivia works.

RestoBar OS is a point-of-sale and operations system I designed and built for restaurants and bars in Bolivia. Waiters take orders on their own phones, the kitchen and bar get each ticket with a timer, and the owner follows the day from anywhere. It costs Bs 350 a month, with no contract.

01 · The brief

The big restaurant systems ask for tablets and their own hardware, two- to three-year contracts, extra fees for each module and their own card processor. None of them issue Bolivian SIN invoices.

Restaurants here run on QR payments, cash and WhatsApp. Owners told me their biggest worries are deleted sales and a cash drawer that doesn't add up at the end of the night.

02 · The approach

  1. Phones, not hardwareIt runs in the browser on Android and iPhone, and each person signs in with a PIN. A restaurant only needs a thermal printer for the kitchen and one Android device for the cashier.
  2. Roles the system enforcesSix roles: owner, manager, cashier, waiter, bartender and kitchen. A waiter can't charge, change prices or void a sale, and the database blocks it, not just the screen. Every void needs the manager's PIN and is logged with a name and a reason.
  3. Built around the ownerA live summary shows sales by hour, best sellers, how customers paid, voids and the expected cash. The full close of the day goes to the owner's WhatsApp each night.

03 · What I built

  • Waiter ordering with photos, options and smart add-on suggestions
  • Kitchen and bar screens with timers, plus printed tickets
  • Sold-out items disappear from every screen at once
  • Cashier shifts with cash counts by bill and coin
  • Attendance by PIN, schedules, tip splits by hours and an export for the accountant
  • Menu editor: change a price and every phone has it instantly
  • Owner dashboard with sales, voids and cash
  • A sales page with pricing, a comparison table and FAQs

Systems like this are custom projects, quoted separately for each business.
