    About

    Hi, I'm Marvz. I design and build websites and apps that earn their keep.

    I'm an independent designer and developer in Santa Cruz, Bolivia. I take each project from the first call to launch day and do the strategy, design, code and copy myself, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

    So far that's live projects: a corporate site for a helicopter operator, a point-of-sale system that runs a restaurant from the waiters' phones, a student information system for a K–12 school, an online shop for a premium beef brand, and cinematic portfolios for student-athletes applying to college.

    How I work

    1. You talk to the person building it. No account managers and no game of telephone. The person on the call is the one who designs and codes it.
    2. Phone first. Your customers will find you on a phone, so I design for one first. That means WhatsApp ordering and booking, apps that install from the browser, and pages that load fast on mobile data.
    3. Every language your customers speak. Most of my sites launch in English and Spanish, and some run in six. Language switching is planned from day one, not bolted on at the end.
